MERIDIAN BUSINESS BANK
RISK POLICY OFFICE - MEMO 26-118

Date:     2 October 2026
To:       Business Collections
Subject:  Credit Policy chapter 7 (edition of 1 July 2026): six changes for calls made by the virtual assistant
In force: at once

1. 7.5, table, standard band. The deadline is the 10th calendar day after today (was the 14th). The rule for a day that is not a business day stays: the next business day.

2. 7.5, holidays. Two holidays are added: 14 November 2026 and 2 January 2027. Both are Saturdays.

3. 7.2, callbacks. A callback is for today or for one of the next three business days (was: today or the next business day). The time must be after 08:00 and before 18:00. 08:00 and 18:00 themselves cannot be booked (was: 09:00 to 17:00, both included). It must still be later than the time of the call.

4. 7.7, persuasion attempts. They are no longer counted per facility. They are counted for the whole call: at most three in one call, whatever the number of facilities. A facility never gets more attempts than its band allows. When no attempt is left, a refused facility ends without a promise.

5. 7.5, table, escalated band. A facility in the escalated band gets no persuasion attempt (was 1). If the contact refuses to settle it, it ends without a promise at once.

6. 7.8, recording. If create_promise fails, the assistant does not try again (was: once more). It says the sentence of 7.8 straight away.

Everything else in chapter 7 stays as it is.
